## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the dignified, scholarly, and vibrant spirit of an Islamic educational institution rooted in tradition while embracing modern educational excellence. The target audience comprises prospective and current santri (students), parents, alumni, religious scholars, and regional academic partners.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutional** structure with **Islamic Editorial Elegance**:
- **Dignified & Trustworthy:** Built upon balanced proportions, calm whitespace, and structured layout cadences that signal religious heritage and administrative integrity.
- **Warm Institutional:** Avoids cold corporate detachment by incorporating warm ivory undertones, emerald hues, and luminous gold accents reminiscent of illuminated Islamic manuscripts and campus greenery.
- **Accessible & Clear:** Optimized for rapid bilingual discovery (Indonesian and Arabic/English references), news announcements, registration portals (PSB), and scholastic achievements.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic Islamic educational identity, nature, and institutional honor:

- **Primary (`#047857` / Emerald):** The foundational identity color, representing growth, vitality, and religious connection. Used for primary buttons, active states, key interactive indicators, and primary badges.
- **Secondary (`#064e3b` / Deep Pine Green):** Anchors headers, footers, dominant typographic hierarchy, and formal institution banners.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706` / Academic Gold):** Evokes distinction, Madrasah achievements, honored notices, and high-priority call-to-actions.
- **Neutrals (`#475569`, `#0f172a`, `#f8fafc`):** Ensure optimal reading contrast across long-form khutbah, school bulletins, and administrative documentation. The warm off-white canvas prevents harsh screen fatigue.

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing modern Indonesian cultural resonance alongside exceptional legibility across both dense news feeds and spacious marketing sections.

- Headings utilize confident semi-bold and bold weights with subtle negative tracking for an authoritative, institutional voice.
- Body text retains generous line height ratios (1.5x–1.6x) to facilitate continuous reading of lengthy educational transcripts, news posts, and registration instructions.
- Metadata and category badges utilize uppercase or semi-bold `label-sm` styles with slight letter spacing to ensure crisp legibility alongside imagery.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured grid layout with a max container width of 1240px anchors the academic portal:

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid system with 1.75rem (28px) gutters and 2.5rem (40px) outer margins. Allows clean division for 8-column main newsfeeds paired with 4-column institutional sidebars (announcements, brochure downloads, social streams).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 1.25rem (20px) gutters and 1.5rem (24px) outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 1rem (16px) margins. Horizontal scrolling carousels are leveraged for photo journals and quick navigation buttons to maximize vertical viewport economy.
- Vertical layout spacing follows an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` to `space-xl`), ensuring consistent card padding and section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean, tactile layering over heavy drop shadows to preserve an airy, disciplined scholarly feel:

- **Surface Layering:** Content cards sit on `#ffffff` surfaces layered above the `#f8fafc` warm canvas, delineated with a subtle `1px solid #e2e8f0` stroke.
- **Ambient Academic Elevation:**
  - **Level 1 (Cards, Lists):** `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdown Menus):** `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.04)` — lightly tinted with deep pine green for brand cohesion.
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Sticky Bars, Float CTAs):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Top Accent Borders:** Important institutional cards (e.g., *Pengumuman*, *Pendaftaran PSB*) feature a 3px solid top border in `primary` (`#047857`) or `tertiary` (`#d97706`).

## Shapes

The interface balances crisp institutional precision with welcoming softness using a **Level 2 (Rounded)** approach:

- Standard buttons, text inputs, and listing cards utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- Feature containers, hero banners, and dialog sheets use `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) for prominent framing.
- Category tags, santri status pills, and floating action buttons utilize full capsule pills (`9999px`) to create distinction against rectilinear image thumbnails.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid emerald `#047857` background, `#ffffff` text, 0.5rem radius, medium weight Plus Jakarta Sans. Includes an icon slot (e.g., download brochure, WhatsApp admissions). Hover shifts to deep pine `#064e3b`.
- **Secondary Action Button:** Border `1.5px solid #047857`, transparent background, `#047857` text. Hover transitions to `#f0fdf4`.
- **Accent/Gold Button:** `#d97706` background with white text, dedicated exclusively to high-priority calls to action such as *Daftar Sekarang (PSB)*.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Small, rounded-pill chips (`rounded-full`) positioned over news thumbnails or preceding article titles.
- Color mappings:
  - *Berita Pesantren / Prestasi:* Emerald green surface (`#ecfdf5`) with emerald text (`#047857`).
  - *Pengumuman Penting:* Gold light surface (`#fef3c7`) with amber text (`#b45309`).
  - *Keislaman / Kajian:* Deep pine surface (`#e6f4ea`) with dark green text (`#064e3b`).

### Article & Media Cards
- White background, 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), 0.5rem radius, Level 1 ambient elevation.
- **Horizontal Feed Card:** Fixed aspect ratio thumbnail (16:10 or 1:1 on mobile) on the left, followed by category tag, 2-line title, date, and author metadata on the right.
- **Vertical Feature Card:** Full-width image header with embedded floating badge, high-contrast title, short summary, and interactive hover lift.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#ffffff` with 1.5px border (`#cbd5e1`). Focused states transition to `#047857` with a matching `0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Rounded corners (`0.5rem`) with clear placeholder typography and optional leading search/filter icons.

### Portal Notice Banners & CTA Blocks
- High-impact panels featuring deep pine background (`#064e3b`) with subtle geometric Islamic ornamentation or clean gradients. Text rendered in pure white and gold accents (`#eab308`).
